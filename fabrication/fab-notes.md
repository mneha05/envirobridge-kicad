# Fabrication notes

- Board: 2-layer FR-4
- Thickness: 1.6 mm nominal
- Copper: 1 oz nominal
- Finish: lead-free HASL or ENIG
- Solder mask: any
- Minimum recommendation for this layout: 0.20 mm trace / 0.20 mm clearance or better
- Smallest critical package: BME280 LGA-8
- Mounting: four M2.5 NPTH holes

## Assembly note

The BME280 is an LGA environmental sensor. Treat it as moisture/heat-sensitive during assembly and follow the manufacturer s handling/reflow guidance. Keep flux and contamination away from the sensor opening.
